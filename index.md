## About Me

<p class="lead">I am an M.S. student in Information Security at Korea University and a researcher at the <a href="https://dfrc.korea.ac.kr/">Digital Forensic Research Center (DFRC)</a>, advised by Prof. Jungheum Park. My research focuses on trustworthy digital investigations across modern systems, with particular interests in digital forensics, AI forensics, incident response, and anti-forensics.</p>

Before joining Korea University, I earned a B.S. in Mobile Systems Engineering from Dankook University and worked as a research intern on digital and vehicle forensics. I have also completed WhiteHat School's core and advanced information-security training.
