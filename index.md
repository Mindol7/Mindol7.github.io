## About Me

I am an **M.S. student in Information Security at Korea University** and a researcher at the [Digital Forensic Research Center (DFRC)](https://dfrc.korea.ac.kr/), advised by Prof. Jungheum Park. My research interests include **digital forensics, AI forensics, incident response, and anti-forensics**.

Before joining Korea University, I earned a B.S. in Mobile Systems Engineering from Dankook University.
